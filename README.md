I enjoy taking complicated, very real constraints and turning them into software people can rely on. I'm a Senior Software Engineer at **Nubank**, with 5+ years of experience across fintech, e-commerce, telecom, compliance, and enterprise products.

I work across the stack, but frontend engineering is where I started: thoughtful interfaces, fast experiences, and accessibility that holds up beyond the happy path. These days, I also spend a lot of time on integrations, data pipelines, automation, and the backend systems behind the screen.

> **My favorite kind of engineering:** solve the actual problem, make the result easy to operate, and leave the next person a map.
